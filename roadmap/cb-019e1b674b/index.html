Step 1

First, we study your business.

Before we send one email, we learn how CashBack Realty makes money, who says yes to you, and why. That's what makes the emails feel like they were written by someone on your team.

1

Your offer

Developers pay a commission on new and off-the-plan sales. You pass up to half back to the buyer after settlement, and it costs them nothing. Simple, strong, and we'll lead with it.

2

Your buyers

First-home buyers, investors, commercial buyers, downsizers. We work out who is easiest to reach and quickest to say yes.

3

Your projects

The builders and developers you work with, and which projects need buyers most. More projects means more people to match.

4

Your partners

Brokers, accountants and buyers' agents who talk to buyers every day. They can send people your way, within the rules.

✎

Two checks before anyone gets an email.

A big list isn't the goal. A clean, right list is. Every person goes through the same path before they are ever contacted.

1

Even a "yes" can be dropped.

If someone says they're interested but we can't see proof they fit, they don't go to you. We'd rather give you fewer people who are real than a long list of maybes.

The emails

Short, plain, and about them.

No long pitch. Each email talks about the other person's world first. Here is an example of the kind of first email we'd write for you. The final wording is written with you.

Example only, not final
To: a mortgage broker on the Gold Coast
Subject: your clients buying new builds

Hi [First name],

Quick one. We're a licensed Gold Coast agency. When someone buys a new or off-the-plan place through us, we share up to half of our commission with them. It's paid after settlement, and it's free for the buyer.

For your clients that can mean $10,000 to $30,000 back, and it doesn't touch the price or the loan.

Worth a quick chat to see if it fits the people you work with?

Cheers,
[Name], CashBack Realty
