The emails

Short, plain, and about them.

No long pitch. Each email talks about the other person's world first. Here is the kind of first email we'd write. The final words are written with you.

Hi [First name],

For your clients that can be $10,000 to $30,000 back, and it doesn't touch the price or the loan.

Worth a quick chat to see if it fits the people you work with?

Cheers,
[Name], CashBack Realty

Straight talk

What could go wrong, and what we do about it.

Few replies

Any new message can start slowly.

We change one thing every week, and review the list, message and offer together in months 1 and 2.

Emails landing in spam

The most common way outreach fails.

Slow warm-up, spare addresses, daily health checks, and a rule to pause first.

Rules on email

Australian law is strict on unwanted messages.

Business contacts only, always about their work, clear sender, easy opt-out.

How the cashback is worded

Law societies have warned about some rebate styles, mainly when lenders aren't told. Your model, paid from your own commission with the contract price unchanged, reads differently.

Your solicitor approves the wording before it goes in any email or partner pack.

The market cools

Sales are strong now, with a lot of new supply arriving.

We don't depend on one group of buyers. Partners, developers and interstate buyers spread the risk.

Partners say yes, then go quiet

Common. Most people are busy.

The partner pack, the monthly note and a quarterly check-in keep you in front of them.
