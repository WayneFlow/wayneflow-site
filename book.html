FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Will this hurt my company's email reputation?

No. We send from separate domains set up just for outreach. Your main domain is never used.

How fast will I see calls?

About three weeks to build lists and warm the inboxes. Replies usually start in the first week after launch. Our 5 day blitz booked 25 calls inside one week.

Why do your dashboards show 0 opens?

We switch open tracking off on purpose. Tracking pixels are a spam signal, so removing them puts more emails in the inbox. We measure what pays you: replies and booked calls.

How much of my time does this take?

One kickoff call and a quick review of the emails before launch. After that you just take the calls we book.

Do you guarantee appointments?

Yes. Once your campaign is live, we guarantee a minimum of 25 qualified sales appointments a month. We walk you through exactly how the guarantee works for your market on the strategy call.

How much does it cost?

A one-time $999 setup and $1,500 a month for the full outbound system: cold email, LinkedIn and X outreach, reply handling and booking. See the pricing page for what is included.

Which industries do you work with?

Marketing agencies, construction software, B2B services, and founder and investor outreach. The machine adapts to your buyer.
