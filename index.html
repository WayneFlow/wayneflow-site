The problem

Most cold email is spam with a first name.

Buy a list. Copy a template. Blast 5,000 people from your main domain. Get zero replies and a domain stuck in spam.

Then you decide cold email doesn't work. It works. That just wasn't cold email. That was noise.

The fix

Every email goes through four gates.

Nothing leaves our system until it passes all four. This is why our emails get answered.

1
The right personOnly decision makers who match your best clients. Every address verified, so bounces stay under 2%.
2
A safe inboxNew domains, warmed up for two weeks. Your real company email is never touched.
3
Words worth answeringShort, plain, written for one buyer. No links, no fluff, one clear offer.
4
The right momentWeekdays, business hours, spread out so every inbox stays healthy.
