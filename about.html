Our story

From the streets of Pakistan to $510K+ in client pipeline.

  1. 2018Two cousins, one laptop

    We grew up together in Pakistan with no network, no investors and no clients. Just a laptop, a slow connection and a feeling that the internet could take us further than our street could.

  2. The hard yearsWe failed. A lot.

    Our first campaigns went straight to spam. We burned domains, wrote emails nobody answered and had months with zero replies. More than once we asked each other if we should quit.

  3. The turnWe stopped guessing

    We started measuring everything: verified lists, separate sending domains, short emails about the buyer’s problem, every reply tracked. Replies came. Then meetings. Then clients who stayed.

  4. TodayEight years later

    Dozens of companies helped, most of them in the USA and Canada. This year alone: 588,000 emails, 12,900+ replies and more than $510K in pipeline for our clients.

“We know what an empty calendar feels like. That is why we built a system that fills yours.”Ali & Asim
