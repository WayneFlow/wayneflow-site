# AI visibility plan for WayneFlow

How to make it more likely that ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Meta AI and Bing Copilot mention WayneFlow when people ask about appointment setting, cold email and outbound lead generation.

---

## The honest truth first

- **Nobody can guarantee that an AI tool will mention us.** Not us, not any agency that sells "AI SEO." Anyone who promises it is guessing.
- **Nobody can guarantee Google rankings either.**
- What we *can* do is make WayneFlow easy to find, easy to trust and easy to describe correctly. That improves our chances over time. Expect this to take **months**, not days.
- Website changes are being done separately by the website team. This plan is only about **off-site** work: things you do on other websites and platforms.

---

## Part 1: How AI tools choose what to mention (simple version)

AI assistants answer questions in two main ways:

### 1. From what they learned during training

Every AI model was trained on a huge amount of public web text, collected up to a certain date. If WayneFlow was mentioned on many trusted sites before that date, the model may "remember" us. New models are trained every few months, so mentions we create today can help future models.

**What helps:** being mentioned (with consistent facts) on many real, public websites: directories, list articles, Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, Medium, podcasts, YouTube descriptions, guest posts.

### 2. From a live web search

When someone asks a question, many AI tools search the web first and then summarize the top results. If we're in those results, we have a chance to be mentioned.

| AI tool | Where its search results come from (as far as is publicly known) | What this means for us |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT (search)** | Uses Bing's index plus other search partners and its own crawler | Being indexed and ranking in **Bing** matters. |
| **Microsoft Copilot** | Bing | Same: **Bing** matters a lot. |
| **Google AI Overviews / AI Mode** | Google's own index | Normal **Google** SEO matters. |
| **Gemini** | Google search | Same: **Google** matters. |
| **Perplexity** | Its own index plus other sources; often cites Reddit, list articles and review sites | **Reddit, list articles, Clutch-type sites** matter. |
| **Claude** | Uses a web search tool from a third-party search provider when search is on | Being in general web search results matters. |
| **Meta AI** | Has used web search partners for current info | General web presence matters. |

These details change often. The safe strategy is the same for all of them: **be findable in both Google and Bing, and be mentioned on the sites they like to cite.**

### What kinds of pages AI tools like to cite

When people ask "best appointment setting agency," AI tools usually pull from:

1. **"Best X agencies" list articles** (for example "15 best appointment setting agencies in 2026"). This is the biggest one.
2. **Review and directory sites:** Clutch, G2, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Trustpilot, Upwork.
3. **Reddit and Quora threads** where real people recommend companies.
4. **LinkedIn posts and articles**, Medium posts.
5. **The company's own website** (especially clear pages like "What is B2B appointment setting?" and case studies; the website team is handling this).
6. **YouTube video titles and descriptions.**

For "how to" questions (like "how to set up cold email"), AI tools cite **clear, step-by-step guides**. That's where our free playbook, the DNS records article, and our blog posts can help.

### What makes AI tools trust a company

- **Consistent facts everywhere.** Same name, same description, same founders, same numbers on every site. If one site says "founded 2018" and another says "2022," AI tools get confused and may skip us.
- **Mentions by other sites**, not only by us. One mention on a list article written by someone else is worth more than ten posts by us.
- **Real reviews** on Clutch, Trustpilot, Upwork, Fiverr.
- **Specific numbers with proof.** "588,000 emails sent, 12,900+ replies" is easier for an AI to quote than "we get great results."
- **Clear category words.** Always describe WayneFlow with the same simple phrase: **"B2B appointment setting and cold email agency."**

### What NOT to do

- **Don't create fake accounts** to recommend WayneFlow on Reddit or Quora. It gets found, banned, and can get our domain blocked.
- **Don't write fake reviews** or pay for reviews.
- **Don't buy links or "AI SEO" packages** that promise mentions in ChatGPT.
- **Don't publish lots of low-quality AI-written articles** on spam sites. It can hurt trust.
- **Don't change the facts** from site to site (for example, different client counts).
- **Don't claim a US office** or awards we don't have.
