# Directories and citations for WayneFlow

A list of business directories and B2B agency sites where WayneFlow should have a profile, plus the exact text to paste.

---

## Read this first (simple rules)

1. **Use the same details everywhere.** Same name, same website, same email, same description. Google and AI tools trust a business more when its facts match across many sites. This is called "consistent citations."
2. **Do not claim a US office.** WayneFlow is registered in Pakistan and serves mostly clients in the USA and Canada. If a form asks for a country, choose **Pakistan**. If it asks "markets served" or "service area," choose **United States** and **Canada**.
3. **No street address and no phone number** in the copy below. If you have a real business address or phone you want to show, add it yourself, and use the exact same one on every site.
4. **Never write fake reviews** or ask friends to pose as clients. Platforms like Clutch and Trustpilot remove fake reviews and can ban the profile. Only ask real clients.
5. **Everything here is free unless marked "Paid".** Paid items are optional. Many sites will call or email you to sell a paid upgrade. You can say no.
6. **Save every login and profile URL** in the tracking table at the end. When the website moves to wayneflow.com, you must update the website field on every profile.
7. **Make sure info@wayneflow.com works** (sends and receives) before you start. Many directories send a verification email to it.

### Link types

- **Follow:** passes SEO value.
- **Nofollow:** doesn't pass ranking value, but still sends visitors, builds trust and helps AI tools learn about us.
- **Check:** we're not sure, or it changes. After your profile is live, right-click our website link, choose "Inspect," and look for `rel="nofollow"`. Write the result in the tracking table.

We don't invent link types. Where it says "check," please check.
