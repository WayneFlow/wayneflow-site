How many booked calls go missing?

RevenueHero looked at 6,428 meetings across 15 industries booked in one week and found a 6.5% overall no-show rate. That sounds small, until you look at the worst industries.

RevenueHero's data shows the problem, not the cure: it didn't test reminders. The messages below are a common, simple practice. Track your own no-show rate before and after, and you'll know if they work for you.

Why people don't show up

  • They forgot. They booked it on a busy Tuesday and it's now Friday.
  • It doesn't feel real. A calendar invite from a stranger with no agenda is easy to skip.
  • Something came up, and moving it felt like more work than just missing it.
  • They're not sure it's worth 30 minutes anymore.

Each message below fixes one of these. The confirmation makes it real. The agenda line shows it's worth it. The reminders fight forgetting. And an easy "reply to move it" line turns a no-show into a reschedule.

The timing

  1. Within an hour of booking: the confirmation, with the agenda line.
  2. The morning of the call: a short reminder.
  3. About 10 minutes before: a one-line nudge with the link.

Message 1: The confirmation (within an hour)

Why: this is the moment they are most interested. A real reply from a real person, fast, turns a calendar slot into a commitment.

Message 1: Confirmation
Subject: Thursday at 10

Hi [first name],

Thanks for booking. You're all set for [day] at [time] [their time zone].

The plan: [agenda line].

If the time stops working, just reply and I'll move it.

[your name]

The agenda line

Why: one line makes the call feel planned and worth showing up for. It also tells them what they get, not what you'll pitch.

5 agenda lines to copy
1. I'll show you how [similar company] went from [their problem] to [result], then we'll see if it fits [their company].
2. 20 minutes: I'll walk you through [the thing they asked about], then answer your questions.
3. I'll bring 3 ideas for [their goal]. You pick which ones are worth trying.
4. We'll look at your [current process] together and I'll point out the 2 biggest gaps I see.
5. Quick plan: your goals (5 min), what we'd do (10 min), questions (5 min).

Why: most no-shows are forgetting. A message the same morning lands when they are planning their day.

Message 2: Morning of
Hi [first name], looking forward to our call today at [time]. I've got [one specific thing, e.g. the 3 ideas for your spring campaign] ready.

Here's the link: [meeting link]

If something came up, just reply and we'll find a new time.

Message 3: The 10-minute nudge

Why: it catches people who are between meetings. Keep it one line. Send it by text or chat if they gave you a number and agreed to texts, otherwise by email.

Message 3: 10 minutes before
Hi [first name], see you in 10 minutes: [meeting link]

If they still don't show

Wait 5 to 10 minutes on the call, then send this. No guilt, no "I waited for you". Make rebooking easy.

No-show follow-up
Hi [first name], looks like today got busy. No problem at all.

Want to grab another time? [Day] at [time] or [day] at [time] both work on my side.

Worked example

  1. You book 40 calls a month and your no-show rate is like education software: 40 x 18.1% = about 7 empty calls.
  2. Bring it to the all-industry average of 6.5%: 40 x 6.5% = about 3 empty calls.
  3. That's 4 more real conversations a month, about 48 a year, from the same calls you already booked.
  4. The cost: about 3 short messages per call, which most booking tools can send on their own.

Quick answers

Should the reminders be automatic?

The timing can be automatic. The words shouldn't sound automatic. Write templates with one personal detail, and send them from your own inbox.

Email or text?

Email for the confirmation. For the 10-minute nudge, text or chat works well, but only if they gave you their number and agreed to get messages there.

What's a good no-show rate?

RevenueHero's average across 15 industries was 6.5%. Track yours for a month. If you're well above that, start with the confirmation and the agenda line.

What if they no-show twice?

Send one friendly rebook message. If they don't reply, move them to a check-back list for next quarter instead of chasing.
