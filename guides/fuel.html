The short answer

Leads are fuel. Inboxes burn them. One inbox sending 30 emails a day for 22 working days burns 660 sends a month. With a 3-email sequence, that's about 220 new people per inbox. Multiply by your inboxes, and order next month's list when you have 25% left.

StepMathExample (10 inboxes, 3 emails each)
1. Sends per inboxEmails per day × working days30 × 22 = 660 sends
2. New people per inboxSends ÷ emails per person660 ÷ 3 = 220 people
3. New leads per monthPeople per inbox × inboxes220 × 10 = 2,200 leads
4. Refill point25% of the monthly list2,200 × 25% = 550 left, about 5.5 working days
Note: 30 emails per inbox per day is a common safe rule, and 22 working days is a normal month. The rest are example numbers. Use your own.

Why lists run dry mid-month

Most people buy "a list" without doing the math. A list of 1,000 sounds big. But 10 inboxes with a 3-email sequence start about 100 new people every working day. A 1,000 list is gone in 10 working days, two weeks. Then sending stops, inboxes sit idle, and the next list takes days to build and verify.

The fix isn't a bigger list or a new tool. It's knowing your burn rate (how many new people you use each day) and ordering the next list before the tank is empty.

Why refill at 25%

  • It gives you about a week. 25% of a month's list is about 5.5 working days of sending. That's enough time to build the next list, verify it and load it into your sending tool.
  • It covers surprises. Verification removes bad emails, so a list often shrinks after you clean it. Ordering early means a smaller-than-expected list doesn't stop you.
  • It keeps sending steady. Inboxes that send every day look normal. Inboxes that stop for a week and then restart don't.

Don't skip verification

The reel says it: this only works if you verify the list first. A raw list leaks. Bad emails bounce, bounces hurt your inboxes, and a burned inbox burns nothing at all. Run every new list through an email verifier before it goes into your sending tool, and keep your bounce rate under 2%.

Order a little extra: Check how many emails your last list lost when you verified it. If it lost 20%, order 20% more than the calculator says.

The first-week trap

The burn rate above is for a campaign that's already running, where follow-ups are going out every day. In the first week of a brand-new campaign, there are no follow-ups yet, so your inboxes send mostly first emails. That means you use new people up to 3 times faster at the start (with a 3-email sequence). Keep extra leads ready for a new campaign's first week or two.

Quick answers

Should I just buy one huge list for the year?

No. Contact data goes out of date as people change jobs. Buy monthly, verify right before you send, and you waste less.

Does this count follow-ups?

Yes. That's why you divide sends by the emails in your sequence. Every person you contact uses up that many sends.

What if I send more than 30 a day per inbox?

The calculator still works, just change that number. But adding inboxes is safer than pushing one inbox harder.
