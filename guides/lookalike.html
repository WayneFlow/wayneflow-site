The 6 steps

  1. Pick 5 customers you'd love to clone: happy, paying and easy to work with.
  2. Feed them into a lookalike tool, like Ocean.io, to find similar companies.
  3. Cut anything that doesn't fit: wrong size, wrong country, wrong business model.
  4. Find 2 to 3 decision makers per company.
  5. Use the customer they look like as your proof: "We help companies like [customer]."
  6. Her sequence: connect on LinkedIn with no note, then a voice note, then a short video.

Describe your best customer with Claude

Prompt
Here are my 5 best customers (name, website, what they buy from me):
[paste]

Find what they have in common: industry, company size, location, tech they use, how they sell, recent changes (hiring, funding, new locations). Then write the filters I should use in a lookalike or lead database tool, and 3 job titles I should target at each company.
Warning: It only works if your 5 really are your best. Five average customers give you 500 average leads.

Quick answers

I don't have 5 customers yet. What now?

Use the 1 to 3 you have, or describe the customer you want in detail and build filters from that.

Email or LinkedIn?

Both work. The rep in this example used LinkedIn. We often run both together.
