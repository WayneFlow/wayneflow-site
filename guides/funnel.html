Work out your own number

  1. Pick your monthly goal: how many new clients you want.
  2. Estimate your demo-to-client rate. If you don't know it, start with 1 in 5.
  3. Estimate emails per demo. Start with a careful guess, then replace it with your real number after the first 2 weeks.
  4. Multiply: clients × demos per client × emails per demo = emails you need per month.
Funnel prompt
My goal: [number] new clients per month.
My average deal size: $[amount].
My demo-to-client rate: [1 in X].
Emails needed per demo (guess for now): [number].

Calculate: demos needed per month, emails needed per month and per day (22 working days), and the revenue if I hit the goal. Then tell me which number I should test first to lower the cost per client. Show it as a table.
The real scoreboard: Reply rate is not the scoreboard. Booked calls and signed contracts are. 100 replies from the wrong people = $0.

Quick answers

Why so many emails per client?

This campaign sold annual software contracts to construction companies, a careful buyer. Faster sales cycles usually need fewer emails.

What improves the math fastest?

Better targeting. Emailing the right decision makers raises demos per email more than any copy trick.
