  1. Write the result as one line: who, what result, how fast. Use the number they count (calls, revenue, hires).
  2. Find companies that look like that client: same industry, size and stage.
  3. One angle = one campaign. Ten angles = ten campaigns.
  4. Put the closest match in line one, run all ten small, and scale the winners.

The 10 angle prompts

Paste into Claude
Here is one real client result:
[who they are, what result, how fast, with the number]

Write 10 different cold email angles that use this result. Use these 10 angles, one each:
1. Speed (how fast it happened)
2. Money (revenue or savings)
3. Before and after
4. Same industry
5. Same city or region
6. Same company size
7. The problem they had before
8. What they tried before that didn't work
9. Fear of missing out (their competitor is doing it)
10. The smallest first step

For each angle: a subject line, a first line under 15 words, and a one-sentence proof. Plain English, no hype.
Warning: Only works if the result is real and specific. Never round up or invent a number.

Quick answers

Can I use a client's name?

Only with their permission. Otherwise describe them: "a construction software company in Texas".

How small is "run all ten small"?

A few hundred people per angle is enough to see which ones get replies before you scale.
