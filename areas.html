Do you work with companies in my state?

Yes. We work with B2B companies in all 50 states. The work is fully remote, and campaigns are set to your buyers' local business hours.

Can you target only one state or city?

Yes. We can build lists for a single state, a few metro areas or the whole country, depending on where you sell.

Is cold email legal in the United States?

Yes. B2B cold email is legal in the U.S. when it follows the CAN-SPAM Act: accurate sender information, honest subject lines and a clear way to opt out. We build every campaign around these rules.
